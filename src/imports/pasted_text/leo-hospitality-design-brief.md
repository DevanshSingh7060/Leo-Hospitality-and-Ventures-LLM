Design a premium, elegant, modern website for "Leo Hospitality & Ventures LLP," 
a hospitality management company (not a single restaurant) that manages 
restaurants, cafés, society clubhouse cafeterias, and cloud kitchens, and 
pursues franchise/business partnerships. Brand personality: Premium, 
Professional, Reliable, Modern, Warm and welcoming, Detail-oriented, 
Trustworthy. Positioning line: "Creating Experiences. Building Hospitality 
Brands." Descriptor: Hospitality Management | Restaurant & Café Operations | 
Cloud Kitchens | Business Ventures.

STYLE DIRECTION (structure/motion reference only, not brand): borrow the 
cinematic, scroll-driven storytelling rhythm of speciality.co.in — full-bleed 
chapter-style sections, generous whitespace, layered/parallax food and 
interior photography, warm neutral palette with one deep accent color, large 
editorial serif or high-contrast sans headlines paired with a clean body 
sans-serif. Do NOT reuse their layout for individual restaurant brand slides 
one-for-one — Leo is a management company, not a multi-brand consumer group, 
so the homepage should read "parent company with ventures," not "menu of 
restaurants."

DELIVERABLES — design each as a distinct high-fidelity frame, desktop (1440px) 
+ mobile (390px), in a single Figma file with a shared design system (color 
tokens, type scale, spacing scale, button/CTA components, form components):

1. HOME
   - Hero: full-bleed premium hospitality photo/video panel, company name, 
     positioning line, two CTAs ("Explore Our Ventures", "Partner With Us")
   - "Who We Are" — concise intro block + Read More
   - "Our Ventures" — feature Café Bodhi Tree and Ryvive Roots Cloud Kitchen as 
     two strong editorial cards/panels (photo-led, not generic grid cards), 
     plus a visually lighter "Future Ventures" placeholder card
   - "What We Do" — 5 services as an icon+label row or minimal card set: 
     Restaurant & Café Management, Concept & Menu Development, Pre-Opening & 
     Setup, Cloud Kitchen Operations, Business Partnerships
   - "Experience" teaser — 2-3 past project logos/names with link to full page
   - Footer with nav, contact, social, newsletter

2. ABOUT US — hospitality focus, philosophy, timeline of experience (2015–2025 
   journey), current ventures, future direction. Include a leadership/founder 
   quote block styled like a trust signal, not a personality cult.

3. OUR VENTURES — two full editorial sections: Café Bodhi Tree (café/restaurant 
   imagery, ambience-led) and Ryvive Roots Cloud Kitchen (franchise-forward 
   language, operational/delivery imagery), plus a Future Ventures section 
   with a flexible card layout for entries not yet announced.

4. SERVICES — five service categories from the brief (Restaurant & Café 
   Management, Concept & Menu Development, Pre-Opening & Setup, Cloud Kitchen 
   Operations, Business Partnerships) as expandable/detailed sections, each 
   with a short capability list. Do NOT include "Brand Development & 
   Marketing" as a standalone service.

5. EXPERIENCE — list/grid of past projects: Lord of the Drinks (Mumbai), Tea 
   Villa Café (Juhu & Malad), M&S The Food Factory, M&S The Dessert Factory, 
   Belge Cakes (event management). Present as a portfolio, placeholders for 
   descriptions pending approval.

6. GALLERY — category-filterable image grid: Restaurants & Cafés, Food & 
   Beverages, Events, Team & Operations, Projects, Behind the Scenes.

7. CONTACT — company details, phone, professional email, map, social links, 
   business enquiry form (Name, Company/Organization, Phone, Email, Enquiry 
   Type, Message).

8. FRANCHISE / BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES — "Let's Build Something Together" 
   section with partnership categories and a prominent Business Enquiry CTA.

9. CAREERS — short invitational copy for hospitality professionals, CV 
   submission CTA.

10. VENDOR REGISTRATION (own page, positioned directly below Careers in the 
    nav) — explain purpose (onboarding F&B/raw-material/supply vendors), 
    vendor categories (Food Vendors & Suppliers, Vegetable & Fresh Produce, 
    Beverage, Coffee, Other F&B Raw Material Suppliers), and a full 
    registration form: Company/Firm Name, Contact Person, Vendor Category, 
    Mobile Number, Email, Address, City/Service Area, Product/Raw Material 
    Categories, Brands/Products Supplied, FSSAI Number, FSSAI Certificate 
    Upload (mandatory file upload field), Other Certifications/Documents 
    Upload, GST Details (optional), Message. Include a visible disclaimer 
    line: submission does not guarantee vendor approval/empanelment.

DESIGN SYSTEM NOTES:
- Palette: warm neutral base (cream/off-white or deep charcoal option — 
  present as a light theme with a single confident accent, e.g. deep maroon, 
  bronze/gold, or forest green — pick one accent family and apply it as the 
  system's single accent token)
- Typography: one editorial display face for headlines (premium, hospitality-
  appropriate), one clean grotesk/sans for body and UI
- Components: primary/secondary buttons, form inputs incl. file-upload field 
  (for FSSAI docs), nav bar with CTA ("Business Enquiry / Partner With Us"), 
  footer, card components for ventures/services/experience/gallery
- Responsive: define how full-bleed parallax hero sections and multi-column 
  editorial sections collapse to single-column, stacked mobile layouts
- Accessibility: sufficient contrast on text-over-image sections, visible 
  focus states on form fields
